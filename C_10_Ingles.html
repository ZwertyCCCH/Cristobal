🌍 Introduction

Why Does Transport Matter?

Transport is essential for modern life. It connects people, places, and opportunities.

🏙️
Urban Mobility
In cities, millions of people move every day. Efficient transport systems are vital for economic growth, social inclusion, and quality of life.
La movilidad urbana es clave para el desarrollo de las ciudades.
🌱
Environmental Impact
Transport is responsible for about 25% of global CO₂ emissions. Choosing sustainable options like walking, cycling, and public transport is essential for the planet.
El transporte sostenible es crucial para reducir el impacto ambiental.
👥
Social Equality
Access to affordable, reliable transport determines opportunities for education, work, and social life. Young people and low-income groups are often the most affected.
El transporte accesible es una cuestión de igualdad social.
📱
Technology & Innovation
From apps to electric vehicles, technology is transforming how we travel. The future of transport is connected, autonomous, and shared.
La tecnología está revolucionando la movilidad.
Think about it: How do you get to school? What do you like or dislike about your daily journey?
¿Cómo vas a la escuela? ¿Qué te gusta o no te gusta de tu viaje diario?

📝 Grammar

Present Simple for Future

We use the present simple to talk about timetables, schedules and fixed future events.

RULE: Use the present simple for events that depend on a clock or calendar — not personal plans.
Usa el presente simple para eventos que dependen de un reloj o calendario — no para planes personales.
✅
Correct Uses
The train leaves at 6 pm.
El tren sale a las 6 pm.

What time does the film start?
¿A qué hora empieza la película?

My class finishes at 12:30 tomorrow.
Mi clase termina a las 12:30 mañana.

The museum opens at 10 am on Sundays.
El museo abre a las 10 am los domingos.
❌
Common Mistakes
I will leave at 8 am. → Use present simple for fixed schedules.
Usa presente simple para horarios fijos.

What time does the film starts? → No "s" on the main verb in questions.
Sin "s" en el verbo principal en preguntas.

The bus is going to arrive at 8 am. → Use present simple for timetables.
Usa presente simple para horarios.
The plane departs at 9:15 am and arrives in Paris at 11:30 am.
El avión sale a las 9:15 am y llega a París a las 11:30 am.
📝 Grammar

Be Going To

We use be going to to talk about plans and intentions for the future.

RULE: Use "be going to" + infinitive for personal plans, intentions, and things you have decided to do.
Usa "be going to" + infinitivo para planes personales, intenciones y cosas que has decidido hacer.
✅
Examples
I'm going to visit my grandma this weekend.
Voy a visitar a mi abuela este fin de semana.

We're going to take the train to the beach.
Vamos a tomar el tren a la playa.

She's going to study engineering at university.
Ella va a estudiar ingeniería en la universidad.
📝
Form
Subject + be (am/is/are) + going to + infinitive

I am going to walk to school.
Voy a caminar a la escuela.

They are not going to take the bus.
No van a tomar el autobús.

Are you going to cycle there?
¿Vas a ir en bicicleta allí?
I am going to hire a bike to explore the city tomorrow.
Voy a alquilar una bicicleta para explorar la ciudad mañana.
📝 Grammar

Will

We use will for predictions, promises, offers, spontaneous decisions, and facts about the future.

RULE: Use "will" + infinitive for predictions, promises, offers, and decisions made at the moment of speaking.
Usa "will" + infinitivo para predicciones, promesas, ofrecimientos y decisiones tomadas en el momento de hablar.
🔮
Predictions
I think electric cars will become more popular.
Creo que los coches eléctricos serán más populares.

The traffic will be terrible at rush hour.
El tráfico será terrible en hora punta.
🤝
Promises & Offers
I will call you when I arrive.
Te llamaré cuando llegue.

I will help you carry your bags.
Te ayudaré a llevar tus bolsas.

Don't worry, I will give you a lift.
No te preocupes, te llevaré en coche.
"I don't have a ticket." — "Don't worry, I will buy one for you."
"No tengo boleto." — "No te preocupes, te compraré uno."
Contrast: Present Simple: fixed schedule (The train leaves at 6 pm.)
Be going to: plan/intention (I'm going to take the train.)
Will: prediction/promise/offer (I think it will be on time.)
Presente Simple: horario fijo / Be going to: plan / Will: predicción/promesa/ofrecimiento.

📝 Grammar

Modal Verbs for Transport

Modals express possibility, necessity, permission, ability and obligation.

🟢
Possibility & Permission
Can / Could — permission, ability, possibility
"Can I get a discount with my student ID?"
¿Puedo obtener un descuento con mi carnet de estudiante?

"Could you tell me the way to the station?"
¿Podrías decirme el camino a la estación?

"May I have a map of the city?"
¿Puedo tener un mapa de la ciudad?
🔴
Obligation & Necessity
Must / Should — obligation, advice, recommendation
"You must buy a ticket before you get on the train."
Debes comprar un boleto antes de subir al tren.

"You should take the metro; it's faster."
Deberías tomar el metro; es más rápido.

"You mustn't use your phone while driving."
No debes usar el teléfono mientras conduces.
You can buy tickets online, but you must print them before the trip.
Puedes comprar boletos en línea, pero debes imprimirlos antes del viaje.
Could you tell me if this bus goes to the city centre?
¿Podrías decirme si este autobús va al centro de la ciudad?
Modal Verbs Summary: Can: ability, permission, possibility
Could: polite request, possibility, past ability
May: formal permission, possibility
Must: strong obligation, rule
Should: advice, recommendation

Sustainable Transport Around the World

Different cities are adopting innovative solutions for sustainable mobility.

🇳🇱
Amsterdam, Netherlands
Cycling capital of the world. Over 60% of journeys are made by bike.
Extensive network of cycle lanes and bike parking facilities.
Electric cargo bikes are becoming popular for deliveries.
Capital mundial de la bicicleta. Más del 60% de los viajes se hacen en bici.
🇯🇵
Tokyo, Japan
World's most efficient public transport system.
Trains arrive every 2-3 minutes and are always on time.
Many people use electric bikes for short trips.
El sistema de transporte público más eficiente del mundo. Los trenes siempre llegan a tiempo.
🇨🇴
Medellín, Colombia
Famous for its cable car system connecting hillside communities.
Integrated with metro, buses, and bike lanes.
Reduced travel time and improved access for low-income areas.
Famoso por su sistema de teleférico que conecta comunidades en las colinas.
🇬🇧
London, UK
Congestion charge reduces traffic in the city centre.
Extensive cycle hire network (Boris Bikes).
Investment in electric buses and low-emission zones.
Peaje por congestión y red de alquiler de bicicletas. Inversión en autobuses eléctricos.

✍️ Writing

Writing a Formal Letter

Write a formal letter to the mayor proposing improvements to transport in your city.

Structure of a Formal Letter:
1. Sender's Address: Your address (top right)
2. Date: The date you're writing
3. Recipient's Address: The mayor's office (left)
4. Salutation: Dear Mayor [Name],
5. Introduction: State why you are writing
6. Body: Describe the problem and your proposed solution
7. Conclusion: Request action and thank them
8. Closing: Yours sincerely / Yours faithfully
9. Signature: Your full name
Sample Introduction:
I am writing to express my concern about the transport situation for young people in our city. Many students rely on public transport to get to school, but the buses are often delayed and overcrowded.
Escribo para expresar mi preocupación por la situación del transporte para los jóvenes en nuestra ciudad. Muchos estudiantes dependen del transporte público para ir a la escuela, pero los autobuses a menudo llegan tarde y están abarrotados.
Sample Conclusion:
I would like to request that you consider investing in more cycle lanes and improving the frequency of buses during rush hour. I look forward to your response.
Me gustaría solicitar que considere invertir en más carriles bici y mejorar la frecuencia de los autobuses durante la hora punta. Quedo atento a su respuesta.
Language Tip: Use formal language: "I would like to suggest..." instead of "I think...".
Use "Firstly", "Furthermore", "In addition" to structure your arguments.
Usa lenguaje formal: "Me gustaría sugerir..." en lugar de "Creo que...". Usa conectores para estructurar tus argumentos.
🌱 Sustainability

Sustainability & Road Safety

Key concepts for responsible urban mobility.

🌍
Sustainable Mobility
What is sustainable transport?
Transport that has low impact on the environment, is affordable, and accessible to all.

Key principles:
• Reduce carbon emissions
• Promote active transport (walking, cycling)
• Invest in public transport
• Use renewable energy
• Reduce traffic congestion
Transporte con bajo impacto ambiental, asequible y accesible para todos.
🛡️
Road Safety
Essential rules:
• Always wear a helmet when cycling.
• Use crosswalks to cross the street.
• Follow traffic lights and road signs.
• Don't use your phone while walking near traffic.
• Be aware of your surroundings.
• Wear bright clothing at night.
Reglas esenciales: usa casco, cruza por los pasos peatonales, sigue las señales, no uses el teléfono cerca del tráfico.
Always wear a helmet when cycling. Don't use your phone while crossing the street.
Siempre usa casco cuando vayas en bicicleta. No uses el teléfono mientras cruzas la calle.
Your commitment: What can you do to make your daily transport more sustainable and safer?
